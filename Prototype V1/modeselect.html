Your ultimate charges as you clear lines — the amount needed depends on your class. Some abilities (like Scramble, Grid Shift, and Guardian Angel) affect whichever opponent you have targeted, so use Tab to pick who you're aiming at before you fire.

Every class has a passive bonus plus a Q, E, and ultimate ability. Pick the one that matches how you like to play.
