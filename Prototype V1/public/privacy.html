Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Academic Context

This Privacy Policy explains how Cascade collects, uses, and protects your information. Cascade is developed strictly for academic research under the College of Computer Science at the University of Makati (UMREC Approved).

2. Data Collection

We collect gameplay telemetry data solely for academic analysis and the evaluation of ISO/IEC 25010 standards. The data collected includes:

  • Match duration, win rates, and class selections.
  • Input timing and game state changes for pacing analysis.
  • Anonymized Supabase UUIDs.

We do NOT collect real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or any sensitive financial information.

3. Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL) protected by Row Level Security (RLS). Local storage is used on your device exclusively to maintain login sessions and cache telemetry for synchronization.

4. Your Rights (Data Deletion)

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), you have the right to erase your data. You may permanently delete your account and all associated telemetry data at any time using the "Delete My Account & Data" button in the in-game Settings menu.

Developed by the College of Computer Science, University of Makati, for academic research purposes.
